Latest Updates and Announcements

Our community continues to grow and thrive with the addition of new workshops and learning opportunities. This month, we're excited to announce several new initiatives that will benefit both beginners and experienced developers alike.

The Web Development Workshop series has been a tremendous success, with over 200 participants joining our first session. The hands-on approach and real-world projects have received overwhelmingly positive feedback. Due to popular demand, we're adding advanced sessions focusing on modern frameworks and cloud deployment.
